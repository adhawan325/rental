## Financing Details

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Loan Amount | $240,000.00 |
| Interest Rate | 6.5% |
| Loan Term | 30 years |
| **Monthly Payment** | **$1,516.96** |

### Calculation:
```
Loan Amount = Purchase Price - Down Payment
Loan Amount = $300,000 - $60,000 = $240,000

Monthly Payment Formula: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]
Where:
  P = $240,000
  r = 6.5% / 12 / 100 = 0.00541667
  n = 30 × 12 = 360

(1 + r)^n = 1.00541667^360 = 7.2446

M = $240,000 × [0.00541667 × 7.2446] / [7.2446 - 1]
M = $240,000 × 0.006283
M = $1,516.96
```

## Yield Analysis

### Gross Yield

```
Formula: (Annual Rental Income / Purchase Price) × 100

Annual Rental Income = Monthly Rent × 12
Annual Rental Income = $2,000 × 12 = $24,000

Gross Yield = ($24,000 / $300,000) × 100
Gross Yield = 0.08 × 100
Gross Yield = 8.00%
```

**Result: 8.00% Gross Yield** ✅ (Good)

### Net Yield

```
Formula: ((Annual Rental Income - Annual Operating Expenses) / Purchase Price) × 100

Annual Rental Income = $24,000
Annual Operating Expenses = $1,125 × 12 = $13,500

Net Income = $24,000 - $13,500 = $10,500

Net Yield = ($10,500 / $300,000) × 100
Net Yield = 0.035 × 100
Net Yield = 3.50%
```

**Result: 3.50% Net Yield** ⚠️ (Below Average)

2. **Conservative Assumptions**

3. **Equity Building**
